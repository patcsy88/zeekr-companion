Public contact points (from the Zeekr Malaysia site, research M3): hotline 1300-88-6868, Monday to Friday 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM excluding public holidays; email customersupport@service.zeekr.my.

Keep the owner's name, VIN, plate and phone number out of this repo. Add them to the message only when sending it.

## Message (email or WhatsApp)

Subject: Question about supported access to my vehicle data (Zeekr 7X AWD Performance)

Hello Zeekr Malaysia customer support,

I own a Zeekr 7X AWD Performance (VIN: [add when sending]). I keep my own ownership records and I would like to read some vehicle data automatically for personal use. I have four questions:

1. Does Zeekr Malaysia offer an official, supported way for an owner to read vehicle data (for example battery state of charge, charging status, odometer, tyre pressure) from outside the Zeekr app? This could be an owner API, a data export or an approved partner integration.
2. If there is none, is one planned? If so, is there a way to register interest?
3. Does Zeekr consider access to the vehicle's connected services by software other than the official Zeekr app a breach of the app's terms of use or the connected-service terms? Could it lead to my account being suspended?
4. Would reading data this way, or sharing the vehicle with a second account, affect my vehicle or battery warranty?

I am not asking for any remote control of the car, only for reading data. Please reply in writing so I can keep it with my warranty records.

Thank you,
[Owner name and contact details, added when sending]

## When the answer arrives

Record it in `docs/research/vehicle-integrations.md` with the date, the channel, who replied and what they said, under R1 and as a new row after M42. Label it `verified` only if the reply is in writing from Zeekr. Do not paste the owner's name, VIN or phone number.
